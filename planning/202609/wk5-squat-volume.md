# Squat — volume slot, wk 5

**TL;DR**

- Back squat **285# × 5 × 5**, E3:00 — ~75% TM, log RPE every set. Expect 6–7
- This is a *volume hold*, not a deload: 25 reps, tonnage level with wk 4
- Banded lateral walk 3 × 20 steps/direction + suitcase carry 70# × 4 × 50 ft/side
- Nothing above 285. The heavy read happens in the intensity slot, 72h later

## Warm-up (~10 min)

- 3 min bike or row, easy
- Glute-med prime, light grade: light-band lateral walk 2 × 10 steps/direction + single-leg
  glute bridge 2 × 8/side, **left side first**. Priming, not training — save it for the block below.
- Ankle rock + adductor rock 2 × 8/side
- Squat ramp: bar × 10, 135 × 5, 185 × 5, 225 × 3, 255 × 2 → work sets

## Back squat — 285# × 5 × 5, E3:00

Run solo. ~75% of the 380 TM.

Weeks 1–4 never ran a set at a known RPE, so this session has one job beyond the reps: **write
down an RPE for all five sets.** If they read 6–7, the TM is right or low. If 285 × 5 reads 8+,
the TM is too high and wk 6 gets rebuilt before anything heavier is loaded.

Reference: 265 × 5 × 8 (wk 3) was 40 reps with no drop-off, and 305 × 4 × 6 (wk 4) held all sets.
285 × 5 sits between them. This should feel comfortably repeatable — if set 5 is a fight,
that's the real signal of the day, not a failure.

**Watch the left foot.** Pressure stays spread across the whole foot; the moment it creeps to the
forefoot, the set is over. Five clean sets beat five completed ones.

## Hip block — 2 items, ~8 min

- Banded lateral walk — 3 × 20 steps each direction, medium band
- Suitcase carry — 70# × 4 rounds × 50 ft per side

This is the one non-squat thing in the cycle and it is prescribed, not optional. 53# suitcase
carries logged "easy" on 7/14, so this steps to 70#. The hip is what gates the 405 — this block
is squat insurance, not accessory volume.

Gate: 70# × 50 ft with square shoulders and no lateral lean → 80# next week.

## What NOT to do

- No working up past 285 because it moved well. The ladder does the progressing.
- No extra sets. 5 × 5 is the prescription; a 6th set steals from the intensity slot.
- No conditioning that loads the legs within 24h of the intensity slot.

## Logging

`workout/` file for whatever date this runs. Required: **RPE per set**, plus any left-foot pressure
drift and which side the hip block felt worse on. Wk 6 reads all three.

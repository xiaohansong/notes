# Squat — volume slot, wk 6

**TL;DR**

- Your program says 330 × 5 × 5. Run **320# × 5 × 5**, E3:00 — −10#, ~81% of e1RM, RPE 8
- 330 × 5 × 5 isn't impossible, it's 25 reps at RPE 9. That's the load that produced 9/3's
  grinding third set, prescribed for five sets
- Gate: wk 5's 305 × 5 × 5 read ≤ RPE 7 across. If it read 8+, run 310 instead
- Banded lateral walk 3 × 20 steps/direction + suitcase carry 80# × 4 × 50 ft/side

## Why −10# and not more

Against the measured 395 e1RM, 330 × 5 is 83.5% → **RPE 9**. One RPE-9 set is training. Five of
them, on the same day the intensity slot also runs at RPE 9, is a week built the way 9/3 was
built — and 9/3 moved the e1RM by nothing while costing a week of freshness.

320 × 5 is 81% → RPE 8. Same session shape, same 25 reps, one notch below the line where bar
speed collapses. It's still a +15# step on wk 5.

**The load in your program was close to right.** The set count is what made the week punishing.

## Warm-up (~10 min)

- 3 min bike or row, easy
- Glute-med prime: light-band lateral walk 2 × 10 steps/direction + single-leg glute bridge
  2 × 8/side, **left side first**
- Ankle rock + adductor rock 2 × 8/side
- Squat ramp: bar × 10, 135 × 5, 185 × 5, 225 × 3, 275 × 2 → work sets

## Back squat — 320# × 5 × 5, E3:00

Run solo. 81% of the 395 e1RM.

**Entry gate:** wk 5's 305 × 5 × 5 came in at RPE ≤ 7 across. Any set at 8+ means the 9/3 fatigue
tail hasn't cleared — run 310 and let the intensity slot carry the week.

**The real target here is set 5, not set 1.** 9/3 showed a set-1 RPE 8 degrading to a grinding 9.5
by set 3 — that drop-off is the actual limiter right now, not top-end strength. If set 5 at 320
reads the same as set 2, that's worth more to December than any single would be.

Whole-foot pressure, left side especially. Drift ends the set.

## Hip block — 2 items, ~8 min

- Banded lateral walk — 3 × 20 steps each direction, medium band
- Suitcase carry — **80#** × 4 rounds × 50 ft per side, only if wk 5's 70# held square shoulders
  with no lateral lean. Otherwise repeat 70#.

## What NOT to do

- No sixth set, no AMRAP finisher on set 5.
- No pushing to 330 because it's "what the program said." The program said it against a projected
  max; this is the measured one.

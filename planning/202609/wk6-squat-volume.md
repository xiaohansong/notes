# Squat — volume slot, wk 6

**TL;DR**

- Back squat **295# × 5 × 5**, E3:00 — +10# on wk 5, ~78% TM
- Gate to run it: wk 5 volume slot read RPE ≤ 7 across. If it read 8+, repeat 285 instead
- Banded lateral walk 3 × 20 steps/direction + suitcase carry 80# × 4 × 50 ft/side
- Log RPE per set again — this slot's job is a steady, readable ladder

## Warm-up (~10 min)

- 3 min bike or row, easy
- Glute-med prime: light-band lateral walk 2 × 10 steps/direction + single-leg glute bridge
  2 × 8/side, **left side first**
- Ankle rock + adductor rock 2 × 8/side
- Squat ramp: bar × 10, 135 × 5, 185 × 5, 225 × 3, 265 × 2 → work sets

## Back squat — 295# × 5 × 5, E3:00

Run solo. ~78% of the 380 TM.

**Entry gate:** wk 5's 285 × 5 × 5 came in at RPE ≤ 7 across. If any set hit 8+, this slot repeats
285 and the +10 waits — the volume slot is meant to be the boring, reliable half of the week, and
a volume slot running at RPE 8 will eat the intensity slot 72h later.

295 × 6 × 4 was wk 3's intensity work (24 reps). This is 25 reps at the same load with a lower rep
target per set, four weeks of adaptation later. It should read easier than wk 3 did — if it
doesn't, that's a fatigue signal worth naming in the log.

Whole-foot pressure, left side especially. Drift ends the set.

## Hip block — 2 items, ~8 min

- Banded lateral walk — 3 × 20 steps each direction, medium band
- Suitcase carry — **80#** × 4 rounds × 50 ft per side

Steps up from 70# only if wk 5's carries held square shoulders with no lateral lean. Otherwise
repeat 70#. Prescribed, not optional.

## What NOT to do

- No jumping to 305 because 295 was wk 3's weight. The volume slot is not where the ladder races.
- No extra sets, no AMRAP finisher.

## Logging

`workout/` file. Required: **RPE per set**, foot pressure, hip-block load and whether shoulders
stayed square.

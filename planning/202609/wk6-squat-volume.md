# Squat — volume slot, wk 6

**TL;DR**

- **Your program says 330 × 5 × 5. Don't run it — it isn't physically available.**
  330 × 5 is 86.4% of your measured e1RM; a true 5RM sits at 86.3%. That's five sets of a 5RM
- Run **310# × 5 × 5**, E3:00 instead — ~81%, RPE 8, a real +5# step on wk 5
- Gate: wk 5's 305 × 5 × 5 read ≤ RPE 7.5 across. If it read 8+, repeat 305
- Banded lateral walk 3 × 20 steps/direction + suitcase carry 80# × 4 × 50 ft/side

## Why the change

On 9/3 you put 325 × 5 up at RPE 9.5. That fixes your e1RM at **382#** — and two other sessions
agree independently (295 × 6 @ RPE 7 → 387, 305 × 4 @ RPE 7 → 378). It's a tight, trustworthy number.

330 × 5 requires a 1RM of 382 to complete **one** set at RPE 10. The prescription asks for five.

For 330 × 5 × 5 to be the RPE-8 session it's meant to be, your 1RM would have to be **407#** —
which is the goal, not the current max. See `cycle-squat-405.md` for the full diagnosis; the
short version is that the back half of your program is written off 405 as though you already had it.

## Warm-up (~10 min)

- 3 min bike or row, easy
- Glute-med prime: light-band lateral walk 2 × 10 steps/direction + single-leg glute bridge
  2 × 8/side, **left side first**
- Ankle rock + adductor rock 2 × 8/side
- Squat ramp: bar × 10, 135 × 5, 185 × 5, 225 × 3, 275 × 2 → work sets

## Back squat — 310# × 5 × 5, E3:00

Run solo. 81.2% of the 382 e1RM → RPE 8, 25 reps.

**Entry gate:** wk 5's 305 × 5 × 5 came in at RPE ≤ 7.5 across. Any set at 8+ and this repeats
305 — a volume slot running at RPE 9 will eat the intensity slot 72h later, and that slot is
where the cycle actually progresses.

+5# looks small next to the +25# the program wanted. It isn't small at 81%: the four weeks of
+20#/week were reclaiming ground you'd already held, and that ran out on 9/3. From here the
ladder moves in fives.

## Hip block — 2 items, ~8 min

- Banded lateral walk — 3 × 20 steps each direction, medium band
- Suitcase carry — **80#** × 4 rounds × 50 ft per side, only if wk 5's 70# held square
  shoulders with no lateral lean. Otherwise repeat 70#.

## What NOT to do

- No splitting the difference at 320 "since 330 was the plan." 320 × 5 is RPE 9 — same problem,
  smaller. The gap isn't a rounding error.
- No extra sets, no AMRAP finisher.

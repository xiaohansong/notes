# Squat — intensity slot, wk 5

**TL;DR**

- Back squat **325# × 5 × 3**, E4:00 — *repeat* of the 9/3 session, load unchanged
- The whole point is the RPE number. Change nothing else — one variable at a time
- Run this ≥ 72h after the volume slot
- Banded lateral walk 3 × 20 steps/direction + suitcase carry 70# × 4 × 50 ft/side
- **Do not** add a 4th set, add load, or chase a 6th rep, however good it feels

## Warm-up (~12 min)

- 3 min bike or row, easy
- Glute-med prime: light-band lateral walk 2 × 10 steps/direction + single-leg glute bridge
  2 × 8/side, **left side first**
- Ankle rock + adductor rock 2 × 8/side
- Squat ramp: bar × 10, 135 × 5, 185 × 5, 225 × 3, 265 × 2, 295 × 1 → work sets
  (the 295 single is a position check, not a warm-up rep — if it drifts, today caps at 305)

## Back squat — 325# × 5 × 3, E4:00

Run solo. ~85.5% of the 380 TM. Rest is up from E3:00 — the wk-4 version may well have been
rest-limited rather than strength-limited, and 4 minutes removes that confound.

**This repeats 9/3 deliberately.** Holding the load looks like a wasted week and isn't: 3 × 5 at
85% is a strong stimulus, and it converts the block's one unknown into the number that prescribes
weeks 7–12. Repeating a load to get a clean read is the same call as holding 315 in May.

Log RPE on **every set**, not just the top one. The shape matters — 7/8/9 and 9/9/9 are different
problems with different fixes.

What the reading buys, in wk 6:

| wk 5 reading | wk 6 intensity slot | why |
|---|---|---|
| all sets ≤ RPE 8, no drift | **335 × 5 × 3** | load lever; clears the 5/16 bail weight, for reps this time |
| RPE 8.5–9, clean reps | **325 × 5 × 4** | hold load, add a set — volume lever, since load is already at ceiling |
| RPE 9.5, a grind, or any forefoot drift | **305 × 5 × 3** | back off and the hip gets looked at before anything heavier loads |

Pick from the reading, not from how the week feels on the day.

**Hesitation = bail.** 335 in May went 2 reps and shifted to the forefoot mid-press — that bail was
the right call and it is why the hip is still available in September. Same standard here.

## Hip block — 2 items, ~8 min

- Banded lateral walk — 3 × 20 steps each direction, medium band
- Suitcase carry — 70# × 4 rounds × 50 ft per side

Same as the volume slot. Prescribed both days.

## What NOT to do

- No fourth set even at RPE 6. The branch table above owns that decision, not the moment.
- No "since it felt light, let's see what 345 does." That is exactly the trajectory that ends at
  a bail, and 405 in December needs the hip more than it needs a September single.
- No CrossFit leg volume within 24h either side.

## Logging

`workout/` file. Required: **RPE per set at 325**, foot pressure on the top set, and a plain
yes/no on whether the bar speed changed between set 1 and set 3.

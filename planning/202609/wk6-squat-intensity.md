# Squat — intensity slot, wk 6

**TL;DR**

- Your program says 345 for five sets of 3. Run **345# × 3 × 4** — same load, one set cut
- 345 × 3 is 87.3% of the measured e1RM → RPE 9. Four sets of that is a real top-end session;
  five is the week that buries you
- Branch off wk 5's reading (table below) before you get to the gym
- Banded lateral walk 3 × 20 steps/direction + suitcase carry 80# × 4 × 50 ft/side

## Why the load stays

Earlier I called 345 unavailable. That was off a 382 e1RM read from the *third* set on 9/3 — the
fatigued one. Read off sets 1–2 at RPE 8–8.5, the e1RM is **395**, and 345 × 3 lands at RPE 9:
heavy, appropriate for an intensity slot, not a rep-max.

So the bar weight your program wanted is right. What isn't is five sets of it in the same week the
volume day also runs hot. Cutting to four keeps the top-end exposure and drops the session from 15
grinding reps to 12 controlled ones.

## Warm-up (~12 min)

- 3 min bike or row, easy
- Glute-med prime: light-band lateral walk 2 × 10 steps/direction + single-leg glute bridge
  2 × 8/side, **left side first**
- Ankle rock + adductor rock 2 × 8/side
- Squat ramp: bar × 10, 135 × 5, 185 × 5, 225 × 3, 275 × 2, 305 × 1, 325 × 1 → work sets
  (the 325 single is a position check. Forefoot drift there → take the 330 row today)

## Back squat — branch on wk 5

| wk 5 read at 325 × 3 × 3 | run today | rest |
|---|---|---|
| all 3 triples ≤ RPE 7.5, no drift | **345 × 3 × 4** | E4:00 |
| any triple RPE 8–8.5 | **340 × 3 × 4** | E4:00 |
| RPE 9+, grind, or forefoot drift | **330 × 3 × 4** | E3:30 |

Run solo. Pick the row from the logged number before the session, not from how the warm-up feels.

345 is 10# above the weight that bailed on 5/16 — but as a triple at RPE 9, not a max double.
**Set 1 is the test.** If it grinds the way 9/3's third set did, take the 340 row and finish the
session; that's a correct read, not a lost week.

**Stop the set when the bar slows, not when it fails.** The whole lesson of 9/3 is that the
grinding rep bought nothing.

## Hip block — 2 items, ~8 min

- Banded lateral walk — 3 × 20 steps each direction, medium band
- Suitcase carry — 80# × 4 rounds × 50 ft per side

## After these two weeks

Wk 7–12 walk the intensity slot 340 → 355 in triples and the volume slot 320 → 335 in fives, all
recomputed off the logged RPE. Doubles at 92–95% land wk 13–15; attempt wk 16 at 375 / 395 / 405.

From 395, **405 needs only +10# of e1RM in 11 weeks** — under +1#/wk clears it. The runway is no
longer tight. What it needs is 11 weeks of sessions that end with something left, which is exactly
what these two weeks are protecting.

## What NOT to do

- No singles. Singles belong to the doubles phase and the attempt.
- No fifth set at 345 because four felt fine. Four is the prescription; wk 8 owns the fifth set.

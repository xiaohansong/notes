# Squat — intensity slot, wk 6

**TL;DR**

- **Your program says 345 for sets of 3. Don't run it.** 345 is 90.3% of your measured e1RM —
  a true 3RM is 90.7%. That's a rep-max, prescribed for five sets
- Run **325# × 3 × 5**, E4:00 — load held from wk 5, two sets added. ~85%, RPE 8
- Branch off wk 5's reading (table below) before you get to the gym
- Banded lateral walk 3 × 20 steps/direction + suitcase carry 80# × 4 × 50 ft/side

## Why the change

345 × 3 needs a 1RM of **404#** to be the RPE-8 working triple the program intends. You have 382.
As written it's five sets at your 3RM — the session either turns into three grinding singles-in-
disguise or it ends in a bail, and 335 already showed what a bail at this load looks like (5/16,
two reps, forefoot drift).

The fix is a lever change, not a smaller jump. Wk 5 puts 325 in as a triple at RPE 8; **wk 6
holds that load and adds sets.** Volume goes 9 → 15 reps at 85% while intensity sits still. That's
a harder session than wk 5 by any honest measure, and it's one you can actually complete.

## Warm-up (~12 min)

- 3 min bike or row, easy
- Glute-med prime: light-band lateral walk 2 × 10 steps/direction + single-leg glute bridge
  2 × 8/side, **left side first**
- Ankle rock + adductor rock 2 × 8/side
- Squat ramp: bar × 10, 135 × 5, 185 × 5, 225 × 3, 265 × 2, 295 × 1 → work sets

## Back squat — branch on wk 5

| wk 5 read at 325 × 3 × 3 | run today | rest |
|---|---|---|
| all 3 triples ≤ RPE 8, no drift | **325 × 3 × 5** | E4:00 |
| any triple RPE 8.5–9 | **325 × 3 × 4** | E4:00 |
| RPE 9.5 / grind / drift | **315 × 3 × 4** | E3:30 |

Run solo. Pick the row from the logged number before the session, not from how the warm-up feels.
The branch exists so a good day doesn't talk you into the load a bad week earned.

Fifteen reps at 85% with clean position is the single most useful thing you can bank in September.
It builds the base the doubles phase draws on, and it does it without spending an attempt.

## Hip block — 2 items, ~8 min

- Banded lateral walk — 3 × 20 steps each direction, medium band
- Suitcase carry — 80# × 4 rounds × 50 ft per side

## After these two weeks

Wk 5–6 give a clean RPE-anchored ladder at 85%. Wk 7–12 walk the intensity slot from 325 → ~345
in triples as the e1RM rises; the volume slot walks 310 → ~330 in fives. Doubles at 92–95% land
wk 13–15, attempt wk 16.

Checkpoint for 405: **380 × 2 has to be real by late November.** Today, 380 is a 1RM. That gap is
the cycle's actual work — closing it takes the full runway, which is exactly why the load can't
be spent in September.

## What NOT to do

- No singles, no "quick test at 345 to see." The December attempt is the test.
- No load above the branch row, whatever the warm-up says.

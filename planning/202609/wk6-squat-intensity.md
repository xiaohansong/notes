# Squat — intensity slot, wk 6

**TL;DR**

- Load is **branched off wk 5's RPE reading** — pick the row, then run it, no on-the-day edits
- Default expectation: **335# × 5 × 3**, E4:00 (~88% TM) — the 5/16 bail weight, for reps
- Run ≥ 72h after the volume slot
- Banded lateral walk 3 × 20 steps/direction + suitcase carry 80# × 4 × 50 ft/side
- This session sets the revised TM that weeks 7–12 are written against

## Warm-up (~12 min)

- 3 min bike or row, easy
- Glute-med prime: light-band lateral walk 2 × 10 steps/direction + single-leg glute bridge
  2 × 8/side, **left side first**
- Ankle rock + adductor rock 2 × 8/side
- Squat ramp: bar × 10, 135 × 5, 185 × 5, 225 × 3, 275 × 2, 305 × 1 → work sets
  (the 305 single is a position check. Forefoot drift there → drop to the 305 × 5 × 3 row today)

## Back squat — branch on wk 5

| wk 5 read at 325 | run today | rest |
|---|---|---|
| all ≤ RPE 8, no drift | **335 × 5 × 3** | E4:00 |
| RPE 8.5–9, clean | **325 × 5 × 4** | E4:00 |
| RPE 9.5 / grind / drift | **305 × 5 × 3** | E3:30 |

Run solo. Pick the row from the logged number before you get to the gym — not from how the warm-up
feels. The branch exists so that a good day doesn't talk you into the load a bad week earned.

**If it's 335:** this is the weight that ended 5/16 at two grindy reps with the load shifted onto
the forefoot. Fifteen clean reps at it would be the single best evidence yet that the hip is no
longer the limiter — which is the actual gate on 405, more than any number in the ladder is. Treat
the first set as the test. If rep 1 or 2 shifts, rack it and take the 325 × 5 × 4 row instead;
that's a correct read, not a lost session.

**If it's 325 × 5 × 4:** load held, one set added. Nothing else changes — no rest cut, no tempo,
no extra hip work. One variable.

**If it's 305:** the week is a reset and the hip gets a proper look before wk 7 loads anything
heavier. Say so plainly in the log rather than writing it off as a bad day.

## Hip block — 2 items, ~8 min

- Banded lateral walk — 3 × 20 steps each direction, medium band
- Suitcase carry — 80# × 4 rounds × 50 ft per side

## After this session

These two weeks end with a real RPE-anchored top set, which is the first honest e1RM since May.
Recompute the TM off it, then wk 7–12 get written: intensity slot walks 5s → 3s from 85% → 92%,
volume slot holds 5s at 75–82%. Doubles phase lands wk 13–15, attempt wk 16.

For 405 to be live in December, roughly **380 × 2** needs to be real by late November. Today's
session is the first checkpoint on that line.

## What NOT to do

- No singles. Not today, not in wk 7–12. Heavy singles belong to the doubles phase and the attempt.
- No "test where I'm at" detour. The December attempt is the test; anything earlier spends the
  attempt without banking it.
- No load above the branch row, whatever the warm-up says.

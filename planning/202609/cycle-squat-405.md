# Cycle — back squat 405# (sole focus)

**TL;DR**

- Goal: **405# back squat**, attempt window early–mid Dec. Best banked: 390 × 1 (5/19, RPE 9.5)
- Working **e1RM = 382#** — measured, not guessed: three logged RPEs agree within 9#
- Squat only. No press/DL/bench ladders this cycle; CrossFit is noise, never advances the ladder
- 2 squat days/wk, ~72h apart: **Volume slot** (5s, 75–80%) + **Intensity slot** (5s→3s→2s, 85–95%)
- 4 phases: consolidate (wk 5–6) → build 5s/3s (wk 7–12) → doubles (wk 13–15) → attempt
- Hard gate: **1–3 wk of doubles @ 92–95% before any attempt.** No 3s → 1RM jump, ever

## Where the block actually is

Weeks 1–4 (8/10 → 9/3) were re-accumulation after the July layoff, and they worked:

| wk | volume slot | intensity slot | tonnage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 255 × 10/9/7/6 | 235 × 5×8 | 17,560 |
| 2 | 255 × 10/10/8/8 | 255 × 5×6 | 16,830 |
| 3 | 265 × 5×8 | 295 × 4×6 | 17,680 |
| 4 | 305 × 6×4 | **325 × 5×3** | 12,195 |

Tonnage held ~17k for three weeks while load climbed 235 → 295, then wk 4 cut volume 31% and
took load to 325. That is a textbook volume→intensity conversion, whether or not it was planned.

**But +90# in four weeks was reclaiming known ground, not building new ground** — and the RPE
data confirms exactly where it ran out. RPE sat at 7 through wk 4 day 1, then hit 9.5 on wk 4
day 2. The ceiling arrived in a single session. From here the ladder moves in fives.

## The e1RM, now measured (RPE added 9/3)

| session | load × reps | RPE | implied 1RM |
|---|---|---|---|
| wk 3 d2 | 295 × 6 | 7 | 387# |
| wk 4 d1 | 305 × 4 | 7 | 378# |
| wk 4 d2 | 325 × 5 | **9.5** | 382# |

Three independent readings inside a 9# band. **e1RM = 382#** and it is trustworthy — this is a
better-supported number than the 390 tested single from May.

Two things follow. First, 9/3 was not a normal training day: RPE 9.5 for five reps is a **5RM
test**, and it was run for three sets. That's why the block felt like it spiked — it did.
Second, the load was never the problem. 325 is an RPE 8 *triple* and an RPE 9.5 *set of five*.
Same bar, different rep target, completely different session.

## The problem with the back half of the program

Weeks 5 and 6 of the existing program are calibrated to two different athletes:

| session | load × reps × sets | vs 382 e1RM | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| wk 5 d1 | 305 × 5 × 5 | 79.8% → RPE 7 | **good, run it** |
| wk 5 d2 | 325 × 3 × 3 | 85.1% → RPE 8 | **good, run it** |
| wk 6 d1 | 330 × 5 × 5 | 86.4% — a 5RM is 86.3% | **not available** |
| wk 6 d2 | 345 × 3s | 90.3% — a 3RM is 90.7% | **not available** |

Week 5 is calibrated to a ~380 max. Week 6 is calibrated to a **405** max:
330 × 5 at RPE 8 needs a 407 1RM; 345 × 3 at RPE 8 needs 404.

**The program is written off the goal as though it were already banked.** That works while the
loads are low enough that the error hides — which is exactly what the first four weeks were —
and it breaks the moment the ladder reaches real intensity. Week 6 is where it breaks.

Fixed: wk 6 volume → **310 × 5 × 5**, wk 6 intensity → **325 × 3 × 5** (load held, sets added).

## Phases

1. **Re-enter at honest loads (wk 5–6).** Intensity slot moves to triples at 85%; volume slot
   climbs in fives. Ladder steps are +5#, not +25#.
2. **Build (wk 7–12).** Intensity slot walks 325 → ~345 in triples as the e1RM rises. Volume slot
   walks 310 → ~330 in fives. All percentages recomputed off the current e1RM, never off 405.
3. **Doubles (wk 13–15).** 92–95% of the revised TM. This is the CNS-priming phase and it is
   non-negotiable ([[feedback_1rm_test_protocol]]).
4. **Attempt (wk 16).** Openers ~365 / 385 / **405**. Target RPE 9–9.5 — a max attempt is not an
   RPE 8 lift ([[feedback_rpe_math]]).

## Standing rules

- **Percentages come off the measured e1RM, never off the goal.** This is the single error that
  broke wk 6, and it will re-break wk 9 and wk 12 if the habit survives.
- **Hesitation = bail.** A cut set at 325 is a correct read, not a missed PR. Bailing costs one
  session; a hip that folds costs the December attempt.
- **Left hip is the gate.** Forefoot drift under load is the known failure mode and it is what
  ended 5/16 at 335 × 2. Any drift on a top set ends the set — not the rep, the set.
- **Log RPE per set.** Every gate in this cycle reads it. A session logged without RPE can't
  advance the ladder, so it wastes the week.
- One variable at a time. If a session struggled, don't hold load *and* add anything new.
- 90 min cap including warm-up.

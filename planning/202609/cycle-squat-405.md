# Cycle — back squat 405# (sole focus)

**TL;DR**

- Goal: **405# back squat**, attempt window early–mid Dec. Best banked: 390 × 1 (5/19, RPE 9.5)
- Working **TM = 380#** — deliberately conservative, gets revised at the first RPE reading
- Squat only. No press/DL/bench ladders this cycle; CrossFit is noise, never advances the ladder
- 2 squat days/wk, ~72h apart: **Volume slot** (5s, 75–80%) + **Intensity slot** (5s→3s→2s, 85–95%)
- 4 phases: consolidate (wk 5–6) → build 5s/3s (wk 7–12) → doubles (wk 13–15) → attempt
- Hard gate: **1–3 wk of doubles @ 92–95% before any attempt.** No 3s → 1RM jump, ever

## Where the block actually is

Weeks 1–4 (8/10 → 9/3) were re-accumulation after the July layoff, and they worked:

| wk | volume slot | intensity slot | tonnage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 255 × 10/9/7/6 | 235 × 5×8 | 17,560 |
| 2 | 255 × 10/10/8/8 | 255 × 5×6 | 16,830 |
| 3 | 265 × 5×8 | 295 × 4×6 | 17,680 |
| 4 | 305 × 6×4 | **325 × 5×3** | 12,195 |

Tonnage held ~17k for three weeks while load climbed 235 → 295, then wk 4 cut volume 31% and
took load to 325. That is a textbook volume→intensity conversion, whether or not it was planned.

**But +90# in four weeks was reclaiming known ground, not building new ground.** 325 is ~85% of
the TM and sits 10# under the weight that caused the 5/16 bail. That rate stops here — extending
it two more weeks lands on 365 (96%), which is the ego-lift trajectory, not the PR trajectory.

## The one thing blocking the program

**No RPE was logged anywhere in weeks 1–4.** 325 × 5 × 3 is either an RPE 7 session (TM is really
~400 and 405 is close) or an RPE 9.5 session (TM is ~370 and 405 needs the full 14 weeks). Those
two readings prescribe opposite things. Everything past wk 6 branches on it.

So wk 5–6 are built to *buy that number cheaply* rather than to add load. This is not a deload —
wk 5 tonnage is ~12,000, level with wk 4.

## Phases

1. **Consolidate + calibrate (wk 5–6).** Repeat 325 with RPE logged; hold volume. Revise the TM.
2. **Build (wk 7–12).** Intensity slot walks 5s → 3s from 85% → 92%. Volume slot holds 5s at 75–82%.
3. **Doubles (wk 13–15).** 92–95% of the revised TM. This is the CNS-priming phase and it is
   non-negotiable ([[feedback_1rm_test_protocol]]).
4. **Attempt (wk 16).** Openers ~365 / 385 / **405**. Target RPE 9–9.5 — a max attempt is not an
   RPE 8 lift ([[feedback_rpe_math]]).

## Standing rules

- **Hesitation = bail.** A cut set at 325 is a correct read, not a missed PR. Bailing costs one
  session; a hip that folds costs the December attempt.
- **Left hip is the gate.** Forefoot drift under load is the known failure mode and it is what
  ended 5/16 at 335 × 2. Any drift on a top set ends the set — not the rep, the set.
- **Log RPE per set.** Every gate in this cycle reads it. A session logged without RPE can't
  advance the ladder, so it wastes the week.
- One variable at a time. If a session struggled, don't hold load *and* add anything new.
- 90 min cap including warm-up.

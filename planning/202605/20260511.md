## 1. Back Squat — Top Heavy Triple

```
1 working triple
3 reps @ 350#  (target RPE ≤ 8.5)
```

**Format change.** The 5×5 volume ladder is retired — peaking phase now runs Mon top triple + Fri top double. Cleaner CNS exposure to grinding loads before the 1RM test.

**Pre-session decision (before warm-ups):** **350×3** is the default open; **345×3** is the bail-down (matches 5/8). Drop to 345 if: 315 single in warm-ups feels heavier than usual, sleep was bad, or the body just feels tapped. A clean 345×3 still beats a missed 350. Pick before you walk up.

Belt and shoes on. 4–6 min rest after the last warm-up single. Recovery from 5/8 is 3 days — the tight spot — so the warm-up ramp doubles as your readiness diagnostic.

**Bail rules — preset:**
- Rep 1 grinds RPE 9+ → drop the bar, re-attempt 345×3 after 4 min rest if you've got it
- Rep 2 hesitates or grinds → bail. Hesitation = bail rule applies. Don't take rep 3.
- Reps 1 and 2 fly → finish rep 3 with full intent.

## 3. Chest-to-Bar Pull-up + Face Pull — superset

```
Every 2:30 × 4 rounds
A) Chest-to-bar pull-up: 5 reps @ BW
B) Face pull (light cable): 15 reps, slow tempo, 1s hold at peak
```

**Pull-up:** **stopping the 35# WPU progression.** It's stalled at 5/5/5/4 three sessions running (4/22, 4/27, 5/4). Pivot to strict BW C2B — chest-to-bar contact, no kip, no momentum. Height is the new progression variable (not load, not BMU). If 4×5 strict C2B is grindy, drop reps to 4×4 — quality over count.

If C2B isn't there yet for 5 reps strict: do 5 strict BW pull-ups to chin-over-bar with the **highest-pull cue** — pull as fast as possible, aim for sternum contact even if you don't make it. 5/9 logged "high pull ups 4×3–4, chest height but chest can't touch the bar" — same direction.

**Face pull:** Combined with Sat 5/9's superset = 8+ weekly sets. Maintenance dose now that the deficit is closed. High elbows, pull rope to forehead, hard scap squeeze. Don't skip — this is the unsexy lift that holds heavy squat under the bar.

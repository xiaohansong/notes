## 1. Back Squat — Supramax Unrack-Holds

```
3 holds (or fewer), full rest (~4–5 min between)
8–12 second hold each, no descent

Hold 1: 395# (~104% e1RM) — the 5/22 target load
Hold 2: 405# (~107%)
Hold 3: 410# (~108%) — CONDITIONAL on 395+405 being silent + stable
```

Unrack, 2–3 small steps back, settle, brace, hold tall. **No squat attempt — just hold and re-rack.** Spotter or safety pins set so you can dump if anything feels wrong.

**410 is gated, not guaranteed.** If 395 trembles, or 405 forces an early re-rack, or you feel foot position drift under either → **stop at 405, skip 410, skip the single.** Don't chase the heavier hold to make the day feel productive.

## 2. Back Squat — Bar-Speed Single

```
1 single
370# (~97% e1RM, ~94% of 395 target)
RPE 7–8 — fast, controlled, NO grind
```

After the 410 hold (if taken) and ~5 min rest. Goal: confirm the descent + drive pattern at near-max load with full bar speed. **Foot position is a graded variable today** — if the heels unload like 5/16, that's a YELLOW even if you finish the rep.

**Skip the single entirely if:**
- Any hold trembled
- You stopped at 405 (didn't take 410)
- Foot drifted forward on warm-up doubles
- CNS feels cooked

## 1. Back Squat — Supramax Unrack-Holds

```
3 holds, full rest (~4–5 min between)
8–12 second hold each, no descent
Hold 1: 385# (~101% e1RM)
Hold 2: 395# (~104%) — the test target load
Hold 3: 405# (~107%)
```

Unrack, take 2–3 small steps back, settle, brace, hold tall under load. **No squat attempt — just hold and re-rack.** Spotter or safety pins set so you can dump if something feels wrong.

**This is the test-readiness gate for 5/22.** Hold quality tells you whether 395 is in reach next Friday:

Honor the pre-committed call. Do not renegotiate on the floor.

## Warm-up (~10 min)

- 3 min easy bike or row
- 90/90 switches × 5/side + leg swings × 10/direction
- Glute-med activation, light grade: light-band lateral walk 2×10 steps/direction + single-leg glute bridge 2×8/side, left side first. This primes left-foot pressure for the top sets — light band only; the heavy loaded block stays after the pulls.
- Dead hang 30s + cat-cow × 8
- Then straight into the DL build ladder below — it's the barbell ramp.
- Before the press 4×6 later: bar × 10, 85 × 5.

## Deadlift — top triple + backoff

Build 100×5 → 120×3 → 140×3, then **155 × 3** top set. Gate to 157.5 next Fri: RPE ≤ 8 and no left-foot drift. Any hesitation on the third pull → rack it at 2 — same call as 160×2 on 7/10, correct read, good data. The ladder passes 160 at triple volume by ~wk 3, which is strictly stronger than today's double.

Backoff 2×3 @ 140kg. If the prior day's WOD was hinge-heavy, cut the backoffs, keep the top set.

## Strict press — 4×6 @ 105#

RPE ≤ 7 every set, no exceptions on a good day — this slot stays sub-failure all cycle (two heavy press days broke the April block). Crisp lockouts.

## Left-hip unilateral block — after all deadlift work

Not the same thing as the warm-up activation: that's light-band priming (no fatigue cost, daily-safe); this is the loaded strength stimulus that produces DOMS and progresses. Same movements, different grade — don't let them blur.

- 90/90 back-leg lift — 3×10/side, slow tempo
- Lateral band walk — 3×12 steps/direction, heavy band, slow

Same movements as your existing hip work — the change is structure: it's logged, and it progresses (ankle weight / heavier band when the left side hits 3×10 clean). Note the left-right difference each week; that gap closing is the metric. Keep ≥48h between this and any home hip-strength session.

## Suitcase carry — 4×50ft/side @ 55#

Level hips is the standard — no lean, no hitching. Gate: all four lengths unbroken → +5#. Doubles as DL grip work; straps stay off the carries.

## What NOT to do

- No 160+ "since it's there" — 160 gets earned back through the ladder at higher volume.
- No turning the press slot heavy.
- No hip block before the pulls — pre-fatiguing the stabilizer under heavy top sets is the exact failure mode being managed.

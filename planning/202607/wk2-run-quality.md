# Run Q — wk 2

**TL;DR**

- **7 × 2:00 @ `9:30–9:45/mi`**, 2:00 easy-jog recoveries — volume advances, **pace does not**
- The week's one job: **all 7 reps inside the band.** Wk 1 ran 8:00–8:49 — every rep out of band on the fast side
- Slot rule: ≥48h from the DL day either direction, not within 24h after a leg-heavy WOD
- CP1 reads this session Sun 7/26

> Wk 1's reps felt like RPE 7 but sat at HR 170–175 — ~90% of true max, near-race effort spent on a Tuesday. The volume ladder advances to 7 reps because the engine clearly handled the work; the pace discipline is what didn't pass, and it's the explicit target this week.

## Warm-up (~12 min)

- 10 min easy jog (`11:30+/mi` — genuinely easy)
- Single-leg glute bridge 2×8/side, left first
- 4 × 20s strides building to rep pace, walk back between

## Work — 7 × 2:00 on / 2:00 easy jog

The band is the prescription the same way 120# is the prescription on the press bar. Running 8:0x reps because the legs feel fresh is the same deviation as loading 140# because it moves well — and the cost is identical: it spends the adaptation budget the ladder needs later (8 reps wk 3, 3:00 reps wk 4). The 2:00 rep ends before the aerobic bill lands — that's the short-rep illusion behind "feels easy, could hold it for 3:00"; the HR trace already answered that question. 10/10 lives at the wk-6 time trial and nowhere else.

**How to actually hit the band:** start reps 1–2 deliberately at the slow edge (9:40–9:45). One glance at lap pace ~45s in, one adjustment — this week the adjustment will mean *slowing down*. Judge by lap average (`9:25–9:50` counts); instantaneous GPS pace is noise. A rep that comes in under 9:20 → take the full recovery at a walk and start the next rep slower.

**Gate to wk 3 (8 × 2:00):** all 7 reps in band AND the last rep finishes with "could do 2 more."

**Valve:** all 7 in band with 3–4 clearly left → log exactly that, and CP1 (Sun 7/26) tightens the band to ~9:20–9:35. That's the legitimate route to running these faster — the checkpoint moves the band; the reps never do.

## Cool-down

5 min jog/walk.

## What NOT to do

- No banking "bonus speed" — out-of-band-fast is a failed session for CP1 purposes even though the fitness is obviously there.
- No turning recoveries into moderate running — they buy the next rep.

## Logging

workout/ file for whatever date this runs — pace per rep, last-rep feel, **in-rep HR range** (not session avg — the 7/16 convention), any outer-hip signal.

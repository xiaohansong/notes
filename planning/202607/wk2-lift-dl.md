# DL day — wk 2

**TL;DR**

- Deadlift: build to **355# (161kg) × 3**, RPE ≤ 8; backoff 2×3 @ 320#
- Strict press 4×6 @ 105# — RPE ≤ 7 hard cap, bail rule below
- Left-hip block (after the pulls): 90/90 back-leg lift 3×10/side + lateral band walk 3×12 steps/direction
- Suitcase carry 4×50ft/side @ **60#** (gate passed)

> 7/14's 345×3 at RPE 7 fired the fast tier of the two-tier gate (+5kg): the ladder moves 155 → ~161kg, which is 355# on pound plates. Rebuild pace toward the proven 425# — the bar decides, not the calendar.

## Warm-up (~10 min)

- 3 min easy bike or row
- 90/90 switches × 5/side + leg swings × 10/direction
- Glute-med activation, light grade: light-band lateral walk 2×10 steps/direction + single-leg glute bridge 2×8/side, left side first
- Dead hang 30s + cat-cow × 8
- Then straight into the DL build ladder — it's the barbell ramp.
- Before the press 4×6 later: bar × 10, 85 × 5.

## Deadlift — top triple + backoff

Build 225 × 5 → 275 × 3 → 315 × 3, then **355 × 3** top set.

**Decision rule:** the 315 build triple is the read — crisp and fast → take 355; slow or the left foot wanders → top out at 350 instead. Both count as the ladder moving. Hesitation on the third pull → rack it at 2: correct read, good data. Gate for next DL day: RPE ≤ 8, no left-foot drift → +2.5kg (360#); RPE ≤ 7 and crisp → +5kg (365#).

Backoff 2×3 @ 320#. **Bail rule:** if the prior day's WOD was hinge-heavy, cut the backoffs, keep the top set — the WODs keep sneaking deadlifts in (40 reps @ 185# on 7/15), and the RPE cap absorbs that fatigue only if the volume stays honest.

## Strict press — 4×6 @ 105#

Load holds. 7/14 broke the cap (sets 3–4 at RPE 9, shaking) — that gets read as context, not weakness: the jerk WOD sat 20 hours earlier and this was the slot's first exposure. But the cap is the point of the slot — two heavy press days broke the April block.

**Bail rule:** the first set that crosses RPE 7 ends the slot. 2–3 crisp sets beat 4 shaky ones; nothing here gates on finishing. Best insurance: don't stack this day right after an overhead WOD.

## Left-hip unilateral block — after all deadlift work

- 90/90 back-leg lift — 3×10/side, slow tempo
- Lateral band walk — 3×12 steps/direction, heavy band, slow

Loads hold — 7/14's read ("have to use knee down, toes only, hard") means the progression gate (clean 3×10 on the left) isn't close yet, which is fine: this is the movement doing its job. Interesting wk-1 read: **right side was harder than left** — the program watches the left, so keep logging the left–right difference every week; if right keeps reading harder, that's a finding worth a note, not a problem to fix mid-session.

## Suitcase carry — 4×50ft/side @ 60#

Gate passed: 53# was "easy" with level hips → +5#. Grab a 60# DB if the KB rack jumps 53 → 70. Same standard: level hips, no lean, all four lengths unbroken. Straps stay off.

## What NOT to do

- No jumping past 355 "since it's there" — the ladder is already moving at the fast tier.
- No turning the press slot heavy — the bail rule runs the other direction.
- No hip block before the pulls.

## Logging

workout/ file for whatever date this runs — top-triple RPE + foot pressure (the two-tier gate reads both), which press sets stayed under the cap, hip block reps per side + left-right difference, carry quality at 60#.
